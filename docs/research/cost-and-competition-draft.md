Note: the `master` branch LICENSE.md (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juce-framework/JUCE/master/LICENSE.md) now references the "JUCE 9 End User Licence Agreement" while keeping the same "dual-licensed under the AGPLv3 and the commercial JUCE licence" sentence. Pin to a JUCE 8 tag if the AGPLv3 + JUCE 8 EULA pairing matters.

**Splash screen — is it required under AGPLv3 / Starter in JUCE 8?** Official JUCE team forum posts:
- "Amendments to the JUCE End User Licence Agreement for JUCE 8" (t0m, 7 May 2024, https://forum.juce.com/t/amendments-to-the-juce-end-user-licence-agreement-for-juce-8/61265): describes "a much more generous Starter tier, with a narrower definition of income" and "the requirement to display a splash screen removed."
- "JUCE 8 Is Available Now" (t0m, 12 June 2024, https://forum.juce.com/t/juce-8-is-available-now/61809): Starter tier has "the requirement to display a splash screen removed".
- Code check (2026-10-31): in the JUCE 8.0.8 tag, `modules/juce_gui_basics/misc/juce_JUCESplashScreen.h` returns HTTP 404 and `grep -i SPLASH` over `juce_core.h`/`juce_gui_basics.h` returns nothing — i.e. the splash-screen mechanism (`JUCE_DISPLAY_SPLASH_SCREEN`) present in JUCE 6/7 is absent from JUCE 8.

Conclusion: **No splash screen is required for JUCE 8 under either the AGPLv3 option or the Starter commercial tier**; the EULA text itself contains no splash-screen obligation. (The AGPLv3 route instead obliges OpenSession to release its own source under AGPLv3-compatible terms.)

### 3.3 GPLv3 ↔ AGPLv3 compatibility (GPLv3 section 13)

Source: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt (plain-text of https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html), loaded 2026-10-31, verbatim:

> "13. Use with the GNU Affero General Public License.
> Notwithstanding any other provision of this License, you have permission to link or combine any covered work with a work licensed under version 3 of the GNU Affero General Public License into a single combined work, and to convey the resulting work. The terms of this License will continue to apply to the part which is the covered work, but the special requirements of the GNU Affero General Public License, section 13, concerning interaction through a network will apply to the combination as such."

Implication for OpenSession: Tracktion Engine (GPLv3-or-later) may be combined with JUCE (AGPLv3) and conveyed; the combined OpenSession work must satisfy AGPLv3 section 13 network-interaction obligations if it is ever offered over a network. Licensing OpenSession itself as AGPLv3 (or GPLv3-or-later) is consistent with both inputs.
